Good to know.

Does it work with my existing notes?

Connect a folder of Markdown files. Notryn follows your folders and reads Markdown links and wiki links between notes. Your files stay in their original location. Obsidian plugins are not included.

Is it free? What about sync?

The local app is free to use. Optional paid sync between devices is planned for later. Sync is not available yet. The app runs on your own computer, without a rented server or Notryn account.

Can I contribute or use the source code?

Visit our GitHub project for the code, issues and development updates. Contributions are reviewed before merging. The software license and contributor terms define what is permitted.
